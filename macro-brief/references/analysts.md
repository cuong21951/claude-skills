# Whose read to look for

A search index, not a citation list. The value here is knowing **which name to
search** for a given question — then you go find what they actually said this
cycle and cite that. Never attribute a view to someone because it sounds like
something they'd say.

**Affiliations drift and this file will rot.** Titles below were verified on
1 Sept 2026. Before a name and title go into a published report, re-verify the
affiliation — it is the single most common error in macro writing. If you cannot
confirm someone is currently active, cite the institution's research team
instead of the person.

### Vietnamese media — which outlet for which data
- **CafeF** — the hub for named-expert commentary; author pages for most of the
  economists above. Go here to find what a specific person is saying now.
- **VnEconomy** — macro-policy analysis, credit and NPL data, GSO/MoF-linked coverage.
- **Vietstock** — brokerage research PDFs mirrored; company and market data.
- **CafeF / Dân trí / Người Quan Sát** — daily interbank rate and OMO reporting.
- **theinvestor.vn**, **VIR**, **VietnamPlus**, **baochinhphu.vn** — English-language
  syndication of foreign-bank forecasts.

### Vietnamese primary sources
- **Cục Thống kê** (Statistics Department) — the former General Statistics Office
  was folded into the merged Ministry of Finance in March 2025. Data still at
  gso.gov.vn. Monthly CPI and quarterly GDP land in the first days of the month.
- **SBV** (sbv.gov.vn) — policy rates, central exchange rate, OMO. **Governor is
  Phạm Đức Ấn** (2026–2031 term; Nguyễn Thị Hồng moved to Vice Chair of the
  National Assembly in April 2026 — a very easy fact to get wrong). Check the
  "Ban lãnh đạo đương nhiệm" page rather than assuming.
- **NIEF** (nief.mof.gov.vn) — Ministry of Finance research institute, renamed in
  2025. Good on oil-price-to-CPI pass-through scenarios.
- **Tạp chí Ngân hàng** (tapchinganhang.gov.vn) — SBV's journal; academic but useful
  on transmission mechanisms.
- **National Assembly resolutions** — the binding growth and CPI targets come from
  here, not from ministries.

### Multilaterals on Vietnam
**ADB Asian Development Outlook** (April/September) has been the most current in
2026. **World Bank "Taking Stock"** is biannual. **IMF Article IV** for Vietnam is
annual but publication can lag — check whether a newer round has been initiated
before citing an old staff report as current.

## Prediction markets

CME FedWatch, Polymarket and Kalshi are worth quoting for *market-implied* odds,
never as forecasts. Three caveats to carry every time: FedWatch runs
systematically higher "no change" probabilities than Polymarket (they agree on
direction, not magnitude); event contracts resolve badly under real-world
ambiguity, which is exactly the case a macro report cares about; and large
traders have measured *worse* accuracy than small ones. Quote the number, name
the source and the date, and never present it as a consensus forecast.
